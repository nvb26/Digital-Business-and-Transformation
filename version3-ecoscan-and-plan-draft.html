The Problem

Sustainable shopping shouldn't feel like research

🏷️

Confusing labels

Unclear sustainability impact

There is no simple, real-time tool in supermarkets that shows the true environmental impact of what we put in our basket.

AI Meal Planner

Personal meal plans that don't repeat themselves

Our AI builds a fresh, balanced and sustainable week of meals — adapting to your preferences, what you've already eaten and what your body needs across the week.

One AI. Three things it gets right.

The meal planner is built around three principles that make sustainable eating realistic for everyday life.

🚫 No repeat meals (14 days) ⚖️ Balanced nutrition 🌱 Lower CO₂ impact
  • ✓
    Variety, not repetition. The AI tracks a rolling 14-day meal history and avoids serving you the same dishes within that window — so you discover new recipes instead of cooking the same pasta every week.
  • ✓
    Balanced across the week. Plans aim for variety in proteins, fibre, vegetables, carbs, healthy fats, vitamins and minerals — distributed across days, not crammed into one meal.
  • ✓
    Personalised to you. The AI learns from your sustainability preferences, dietary needs, allergies, budget, household size and shopping behaviour.
  • ✓
    Sustainable by default. Recipes prioritise seasonal produce, lower-impact proteins and ingredients with strong sustainability scores.
⚠ Note: EcoScan & Plan provides general nutritional and sustainability guidance only. It does not replace professional medical or dietary advice.

Responsibility · Ethics · Privacy

Identified risks & how we control them

Building responsibly means being upfront about the risks. Click any risk to see the concrete control measures we take to reduce it.

The app processes personal data such as shopping behaviour, dietary preferences and usage data. Non-compliance with GDPR can lead to fines, lawsuits and loss of user trust.

Control measures
  • GDPR compliance from day one
  • EU-based cloud services
  • Data Processing Agreements (DPAs) with partners
  • Privacy-by-design principles in product and data flows

Loss of sensitive user data through breaches, leaks or attacks could damage trust and trigger regulatory consequences.

Control measures
  • Secure data storage with encryption at rest and in transit
  • Multi-factor authentication for staff and admin access
  • Regular third-party security audits
  • Incident response plan and clear breach-notification process

AI scoring or meal recommendations could be inaccurate or biased toward certain brands, diets or cultural cuisines, undermining trust and fairness.

Control measures
  • Trusted, audited sustainability databases as the data backbone
  • Regular bias audits of models and outputs
  • Diverse and representative datasets
  • Human review for high-impact decisions and edge cases

Wrong sustainability scores or paid influence over rankings would damage credibility and could mislead users — the very opposite of our mission.

Control measures
  • Independent scoring methodology, not influenced by brands
  • Clear sponsored-content labels where present
  • No paid influence on sustainability scores or rankings
  • Transparent score breakdowns visible to every user

Running AI models consumes energy and water. A sustainability app should not undermine its own message through high computational impact.

Control measures
  • Use the smallest model fit for purpose
  • Host on cloud regions powered by renewable energy
  • Cache and batch AI calls where possible to reduce compute
  • Track and report our own footprint as we scale

Shopping behaviour, sustainability preferences and usage data are sensitive — especially when combined. They must be protected and never used in ways the user did not agree to.

Control measures
  • Clear, plain-language consent flows
  • Aggregated and anonymised data for any B2B insights
  • User-facing controls to view, export and delete data
  • No selling of identifiable personal data to third parties
Our commitment to trust

Sustainability scores belong to users — not to brands.

We're building EcoScan & Plan on a simple principle: the score in the app is the score, full stop. No brand can pay for a better one.

✓

Sustainability scores remain independent and cannot be influenced by brands.

✓

Product rankings are based on objective data and AI analysis.

✓

Partnerships do not affect recommendations.

✓

Sponsored content is always clearly labelled as such.

✓

Brands cannot pay for better scores or visibility.

✓

This guarantees transparency and helps prevent greenwashing.

Data Transparency

What data we use — and why

We only collect data that improves the product for users. Here's exactly what we use and the purpose behind it.

📥 Data we use

The data we process to make EcoScan & Plan work for each user:

  • Shopping behaviour (scans, frequency, categories)
  • Sustainability preferences (e.g. CO₂ priority, packaging)
  • Usage data (sessions, features used)
  • Product and meal data (saved items, plans)
  • User accounts and feedback
  • Payment data — only where relevant for future B2B or paid features

🎯 Why we use it

Every data point exists to improve the user experience — not to be sold:

  • Improve personal recommendations
  • Build better, more varied meal plans
  • Make sustainability scores more accurate over time
  • Generate smarter, more relevant shopping lists
  • Improve the product based on real-world feedback
  • Generate aggregated, privacy-safe insights for partners — never identifiable personal data
